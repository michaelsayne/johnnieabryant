Johnnie A. Bryant

Johnnie A. Bryant is a licensed clinical social worker based in Manasquan, New Jersey, with experience supporting children, teens, adults, and older adults. His work focuses on concerns such as anxiety, depression, coping skills, stress, and life changes through in-person and online counseling.

About the Approach

The approach described on this site focuses on understanding a person’s concerns, identifying goals, and building practical coping skills. Sessions may include discussion, planning, reflection, and support based on the client’s needs.

How sessions are approached

Sessions may include coping strategies, reflection, emotional processing, and goal-setting depending on each person’s needs.

Free Client Openings

A limited number of free support slots may be available. These openings are intended for people experiencing stress, anxiety, depression, grief, adjustment issues, or coping difficulties who may not currently be able to afford care.

Who this may help

  • People feeling overwhelmed or stuck
  • People managing anxiety or sadness
  • People facing stress, loss, or transition
  • People who need emotional support and coping tools

What to expect

  • A respectful first conversation
  • A discussion of your goals and concerns
  • Supportive care planning
  • Referral guidance if another service is a better fit

Important limits

  • Free openings are limited
  • This is not emergency care
  • Availability is not guaranteed
  • Some needs may require specialized or higher-level care

Areas of Support

These are common concerns that may be discussed in counseling.

Anxiety & stress

Support for worry, racing thoughts, pressure, avoidance, burnout, and feeling constantly on edge.

Depression & low mood

Support for sadness, low motivation, isolation, hopelessness, and difficulty feeling like yourself.

Coping skills

Practical tools for emotional regulation, decision-making, relationships, conflict, and daily challenges.

Life transitions

Support during changes involving family, school, work, identity, relationships, grief, or direction in life.

Children, teens & adults

Support may be available across age groups depending on fit, needs, consent, and clinical appropriateness.

Online & in person

Sessions may be available through telehealth or in-person care in the Manasquan, New Jersey area.
